Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pull-Up Tracker free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads and no subscription. All core features are included — logging, goals, streaks, stats, records, and JSON backup. An optional one-time Pro upgrade adds CSV export.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for — install the app and log your first set. Your data stays on your device and, on iOS, in your personal iCloud.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, completely. Every feature works offline. On iOS, iCloud sync simply catches up the next time you’re online.

Is there an Android version?

Yes — Pull-Up Tracker is on Google Play with the same core features. Widgets, Siri, and iCloud sync are currently iOS-only.

Can I move my history between iPhone and Android?

Yes. Export a JSON backup on one device and import it on the other — the format is fully compatible between the iOS and Android apps, so switching phones never means starting over.

What does the Pro upgrade include?

Pro is a single one-time purchase — not a subscription — that unlocks CSV export for spreadsheet analysis. It can be restored on any device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.
